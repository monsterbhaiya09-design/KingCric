This page is the hosted version of the KingCric privacy policy. It is a plain-language policy written for this app's actual behaviour. It is not legal advice — it should be reviewed before submission if the app ever adds accounts, analytics, ads, or payments.

1. Summary

KingCric is a cricket scores and statistics app. It collects no personal data. It requires no account, no sign-in, and no name, email, phone number, contacts, location, or files from you. Your favourites and app preferences live only on your device. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

2. What we collect

Nothing personal. Specifically, KingCric does NOT collect, store, or share any of the following:

  • Your name, email address, phone number, or any account identifier.
  • Your contacts, call history, messages, photos, or files.
  • Your precise or approximate location.
  • Your device advertising identifier (no ads SDK is in the current build).
  • Your browsing history or activity in other apps.

KingCric has no user accounts and no server-side user database. There is no account to create, and therefore no account data to leak, sell, or delete.

3. What stays on your device

The following are stored only on your device (via the app's local preferences storage) and are never sent to us or to any third party:

  • Favourites: the teams and players you follow.
  • Preferences: your language choice (Hindi / English) and any notification preferences you set in the app.
  • Cache: recently fetched match/score data, kept so the app can show you the last verified data when you are offline. The cache is temporary and is cleared when you clear the app's data or uninstall.

Because this data never leaves your device, we cannot see it, back it up, or restore it for you.

5. Data sources and third-party services

Cricket data providers

Live scores, scorecards, series, and fixtures come from documented free cricket data provider APIs — primarily CricAPI / CricketData.org, with free backstop providers. Deep player statistics are calculated by KingCric from Cricsheet open ball-by-ball data and labelled in the app with the dataset size (e.g. "Real • Cricsheet • 420 matches").

When the app fetches data, it sends the provider only what the provider needs to answer — a match ID, a player ID, or a search term. It sends no personal data. As with any internet request, the provider's server necessarily sees the connecting device's IP address when it receives the request; KingCric does not add any identifier to that request. Each provider's own privacy policy governs its handling of such requests.

KingCric never scrapes Cricbuzz, Cricinfo, Crex, Cricmetrics, or any other app or website. It uses only documented APIs and open data.

What KingCric does NOT use

  • No analytics SDK. KingCric does not measure or report your usage.
  • No crash-reporting SDK. Crash reports are not collected.
  • No push-notification service (FCM). There is no background messaging in the current build.
  • No AdMob / no ads SDK in the current build. The current build shows no advertisements — its ad placeholder renders nothing.

If ads are ever added (AdMob)

If a future version enables Google AdMob, that version will:

  • Declare "Contains ads: Yes" in the Play Store listing before release.
  • Update this policy to state that Google AdMob may collect device and advertising identifiers as described in Google's own policies.
  • Keep all other statements in this policy unchanged (still no account, still no personal data collected by KingCric itself).

Until such an update is published here, treat this policy's statement as authoritative: no ads, no AdMob, no advertising identifiers.

6. Predictions, betting, and gambling

KingCric's match predictions are statistical models (recent form, vs-opponent record, venue, matchup, pitch) shown for information and entertainment only. They are not betting advice, not a guarantee, and not a promise of any outcome.

KingCric does not offer, facilitate, or promote betting, gambling, or wagering of any kind. It contains no betting features, no odds, and no links to betting services.

7. Children

KingCric is a general-audience sports-scores app. Because it collects no personal data from anyone — including children — there is no children's data to protect, retain, or delete. The app's content is sports scores and statistics; it contains no gambling, no chat, and no user-generated content.

8. Data retention and deletion

  • Server-side: nothing is retained, because nothing is collected.
  • On-device: favourites, preferences, and cached match data remain on your device until you clear the app's data or uninstall the app, which removes them.
  • No deletion request is needed — there is no account and no server-side personal data to delete. If you contact us about data, we will confirm the above and close the request.

9. Data safety (Play Store data-safety form)

Consistent with this policy, KingCric's Play Store data-safety declaration is:

  • Personal data collected: none.
  • Data shared with third parties: none (provider API calls carry no personal data — see §5).
  • Advertising ID: not collected (no ads SDK in the current build).
  • Data encrypted in transit: all provider API calls use HTTPS.
  • Account / data deletion: not applicable — no account, no server-side data; uninstall removes on-device data.

If AdMob, analytics, crash reporting, or push notifications are ever added, the data-safety form and this policy will be updated before that version is released.

10. Security

KingCric sends no personal data over the network, so there is no personal data in transit to intercept. Provider API calls use HTTPS. API keys used for provider calls are build-time configuration, never user data, and are never shown in the app.

11. Changes to this policy

Material changes (for example, adding ads, analytics, or notifications) will be reflected here before the version that introduces them is released. The "Last updated" date at the top records the latest change.
